# The Quiet Draft

Good writing starts when the noise stops. **FocusEdit** keeps the sentence you are writing bright and lets everything else step back — so the page feels like a single sheet of paper, not a dashboard.

## Morning pages

Three pages, longhand style, first thing in the morning. No editing, no rereading, *no judgement*. The point is not the text; the point is the habit.

- Write before you check your phone
- Keep the pen — or the cursor — moving
- Stop at three pages, even mid-sentence

> "You can't use up creativity. The more you use, the more you have."
> — Maya Angelou
