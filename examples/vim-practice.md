# Vim practice

Open this file, turn on Modus ▸ Vim Mode (learning) and follow VIM_GUIDE.md.
Every line below is a small exercise. Undo with u whenever you like.

## Lesson 1 — move and fix

The quick brown fox jumpss over the lazy dog.
Delete the extra s above with x. Move with h j k l, not the arrows.

## Lesson 2 — words and lines

one two three four five six seven eight nine ten
Jump between the words with w, b and e. Delete three of them with 3dw.
Add a full stop at the end of this line with A
Open a new line below this one with o and write anything

## Lesson 3 — operator + motion

delete to the end of this line from here: REMOVE ME PLEASE
change this WORD into another one with cw
copy this line with yy and put it below with p

## Lesson 4 — find and jump

name, email, phone, address, city, country
Jump to each comma with f, and then ; — delete up to the next comma with dt,

## Lesson 5 — text objects

He said "change the words inside these quotes" and left.
call(first, second, third) — empty the parentheses with di(
Change this word: ciw anywhere inside it.

## Lesson 6 — repeat

TODO fix one
TODO fix two
TODO fix three
Change the first TODO to DONE with cw, then j0 . on the next lines.

## Lesson 7 — visual mode

Select these three lines with V j j
and indent them with >
or delete them with d.
